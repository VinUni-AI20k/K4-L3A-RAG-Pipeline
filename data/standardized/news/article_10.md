# Academic Vocabulary & Advanced Grammatical Structures for IELTS Writing Band 7.5+

To achieve Band 7.5 or higher in Lexical Resource (LR) and Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA), candidates must demonstrate precise collocations, sophisticated thematic vocabulary, and flexible sentence structures.

## 1. High-Scoring Academic Grammar Structures

### Structure 1: Inversion for Emphasis
* *Standard Sentence:* Governments rarely invest sufficiently in public healthcare.
* *Inverted Sentence (Band 8+):* **Rarely do governments allocate sufficient resources to public healthcare infrastructure.**
* *Other Inversion Openers:* *Not only is higher education vital, but it also...*, *Seldom have societies witnessed such rapid technological upheaval.*, *Under no circumstances should children be permitted to...*

### Structure 2: Mixed Conditionals & Inverted Conditionals
* *Standard Conditional:* If the government had acted earlier, the crisis would not be so severe today.
* *Inverted Conditional (Band 8.5+):* **Had authorities implemented proactive regulations earlier, the ongoing ecological degradation could have been substantially mitigated.**
* *Present Inverted Conditional:* **Should authorities fail to curb carbon emissions, irreversible consequences will inevitably ensue.**

### Structure 3: Participle Clauses (Condensing Information Elegantly)
* *Standard:* When citizens consume fast food regularly, they increase their risk of cardiovascular disorders.
* *Participle Clause:* **Consuming processed foods on a frequent basis, individuals inadvertently elevate their susceptibility to severe cardiovascular ailments.**
* *Passive Participle:* **Driven by the relentless pursuit of profit, many corporations neglect environmental preservation.**

### Structure 4: Cleft Sentences (Focusing Attention)
* *Standard:* Technological addiction causes social isolation among youth.
* *Cleft Sentence:* **It is the pervasive dependence on digital gadgets that primarily exacerbates youth alienation and sedentary lifestyles.**
* *Pseudo-cleft:* **What is urgently required is a comprehensive overhaul of the outdated national curriculum.**

## 2. Topic-Specific Collocation Banks for Task 2

### Topic A: Technology & Artificial Intelligence
* *Collocations:* break-neck technological advancement, disrupt conventional employment sectors, algorithmic bias, automated decision-making, digital literacy, human cognitive faculties, ethical dilemmas, surveillance state.
* *Model Sentence:* *The unprecedented proliferation of artificial intelligence threatens to displace blue-collar and clerical workers alike, underscoring the pressing necessity for vocational reskilling.*

### Topic B: Environment & Sustainability
* *Collocations:* ecological degradation, carbon-neutral initiatives, deplete non-renewable resources, renewable energy alternatives, irreversible climate catastrophe, environmental footprint, enforce stringent penalties on polluters.
* *Model Sentence:* *Subsidizing clean energy technologies offers a viable pathway toward decoupling economic growth from environmental degradation.*

### Topic C: Education & Employment
* *Collocations:* foster critical thinking proficiencies, well-rounded development, academic credentials, vocational apprenticeship programs, bridging the skills deficit, white-collar workforce, rote memorization, tailor educational curricula.
* *Model Sentence:* *Prioritizing practical vocational apprenticeships over theoretical rote learning empowers school leavers to integrate smoothly into competitive modern labor markets.*

### Topic D: Society, Urbanization & Crime
* *Collocations:* socioeconomic disparities, demographic aging, juvenile delinquency, custodial sentencing (prison), non-custodial rehabilitation, urban sprawl, affordable social housing, reintegrate offenders into society.
* *Model Sentence:* *Rather than relying solely on punitive custodial sentences, progressive legal frameworks emphasize rehabilitative counseling to permanently deter recurrent criminal behavior.*
