# IELTS Writing Task 2 Complete Blueprint: The 5 Essay Types, Structures, and Band 8.5+ Model Essays

IELTS Writing Task 2 accounts for 66.7% (two-thirds) of your total Writing score. You must write an academic essay of at least 250 words within approximately 40 minutes. Mastering the specific requirements of each essay prompt type is essential for Band 7.5 to 9.0.

## 1. The Five Main Essay Types in Task 2

### Type 1: Opinion Essay (Agree or Disagree)
* **Question Prompt:** *To what extent do you agree or disagree?* or *Do you agree or disagree?*
* **Key Strategy:** Decide on your opinion during planning and maintain that exact position throughout the entire essay from Introduction to Conclusion.
* **Structure (Strong One-Sided Approach):**
  * Intro: Paraphrase topic + Direct Thesis statement (*I completely agree with this viewpoint because...*).
  * Body 1: First compelling reason supporting your stance (Topic sentence + Explanation + Concrete Example).
  * Body 2: Second compelling reason supporting your stance (Topic sentence + Elaboration + Impact).
  * Conclusion: Restate thesis using fresh vocabulary + summarize main reasons.

### Type 2: Discussion Essay (Discuss Both Views and Give Your Opinion)
* **Question Prompt:** *Discuss both views and give your opinion.*
* **Crucial Rule:** You MUST give equal weight to discussing both viewpoints before stating or reiterating your own opinion. Failing to discuss both sides limits your Task Response score to Band 5 or 6.
* **Structure:**
  * Intro: Paraphrase both perspectives + State your personal stance clearly.
  * Body 1: Analyze View A (Explain why proponents hold this view + supportive evidence).
  * Body 2: Analyze View B (Explain the counter-arguments and why you find this side more persuasive).
  * Conclusion: Synthesize both views + reiterate your concluding opinion.

### Type 3: Advantages and Disadvantages
* **Prompt Variant A:** *Discuss the advantages and disadvantages.* (Neutral discussion, no opinion required unless asked).
* **Prompt Variant B:** *Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?* (Opinion required: explicitly declare which side is stronger).
* **Structure (for Outweigh):**
  * Intro: Paraphrase topic + State clearly that benefits surpass drawbacks (or vice versa).
  * Body 1: Acknowledge the lesser side (e.g., genuine drawbacks).
  * Body 2: Present the dominant side with greater depth, significance, and long-term impacts.
  * Conclusion: Summarize the contrast and reaffirm the verdict.

### Type 4: Problem and Solution (or Causes and Solutions)
* **Question Prompt:** *What are the causes of this problem, and what solutions can be suggested?*
* **Structure:**
  * Intro: Paraphrase the issue + Outline that serious causes exist and urgent solutions are required.
  * Body 1: 1-2 major causes (Explain root mechanisms, e.g., economic pressure or educational gaps).
  * Body 2: Practical, actionable solutions corresponding directly to the causes discussed in Body 1.
  * Conclusion: Summarize causes and state that joint action (governments + individuals) is needed.

### Type 5: Direct Question / Two-Part Question
* **Question Prompt:** Two distinct questions asked in one prompt (e.g., *Why is this the case? Is this a positive or negative development?*).
* **Structure:** Dedicate Body Paragraph 1 entirely to answering Question 1, and Body Paragraph 2 entirely to answering Question 2.

## 2. The P.E.E.L Body Paragraph Method for Band 8+

Each body paragraph must present a clear, fully developed central topic using the P.E.E.L framework:
* **Point (Topic Sentence):** State the main claim clearly in the opening sentence.
* **Explanation:** Explain *how* and *why* this claim is valid in 1-2 sentences.
* **Example:** Provide a realistic, logical scenario or study that substantiates the explanation.
* **Link:** Conclude the paragraph by linking the point directly back to the essay prompt.

## 3. Band 9 Model Essay & Examiner Breakdown

**Prompt:** *Some people believe that unpaid community service should be a compulsory part of high school programmes. To what extent do you agree or disagree?*

**Band 9 Model Essay (298 words):**
It is argued by some that secondary school students should be mandated to undertake unpaid voluntary work as an obligatory component of their academic curriculum. I wholly agree with this proposition because community service fosters essential social empathy and equips adolescents with invaluable practical life skills.

To begin with, participating in voluntary activities cultivates civic duty and social consciousness among young people. Modern high school curricula tend to be heavily academic, which often insulates pupils from real-world societal problems such as poverty, elderly loneliness, and environmental degradation. By engaging in charitable endeavors, such as aiding food banks or assisting in hospices, students gain direct exposure to the hardships encountered by marginalized demographics. This hands-on experience nurtures emotional intelligence and helps dismantle self-centered tendencies, ultimately shaping teenagers into compassionate and socially responsible citizens.

Furthermore, mandatory community service provides adolescents with practical competence that conventional classrooms fail to deliver. Academic qualifications alone are frequently insufficient in preparing youngsters for the professional workplace. Through volunteer programs, pupils learn teamwork, conflict resolution, time management, and verbal communication in authentic environments. For instance, organizing a community recycling initiative demands logistical coordination, leadership, and public engagement. These interpersonal proficiencies significantly bolster a student's resume, providing a tangible competitive advantage when applying for tertiary education or entering the job market.

In conclusion, I firmly support the inclusion of compulsory unpaid community service in secondary schooling. This policy not only instills moral responsibility and communal compassion in youngsters but also endows them with indispensable practical skills that ensure both personal maturity and future occupational success.
