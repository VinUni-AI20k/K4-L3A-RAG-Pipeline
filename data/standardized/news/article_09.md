# IELTS Writing Marking Criteria In-Depth: Examiner Secrets, Band Descriptors & Scoring Band 7.0 to 9.0

IELTS Writing is marked by certified examiners using four equally weighted criteria (25% each). Understanding exactly how examiners assign marks across the four criteria is the key to breaking through from Band 6.0/6.5 to Band 7.5+.

## Criterion 1: Task Response (TR - Task 2) & Task Achievement (TA - Task 1)

* **What Examiners Look For:**
  * Did the candidate answer every single part of the prompt?
  * Is there a clear, sustained position throughout the essay?
  * Are the main ideas fully extended, explained, and supported with relevant reasoning?
* **The Band Score Thresholds:**
  * **Band 5:** Addresses the prompt only partially; format may be inappropriate; expresses a position but the development is not clear; ideas are limited or unsupported.
  * **Band 6:** Addresses all parts of the task, but some parts may be more fully covered than others; presents a relevant position though the conclusion may become unclear; main ideas are relevant but may be over-generalized.
  * **Band 7:** Fully addresses all parts of the task; presents a clear position throughout; presents, extends, and supports main ideas, though there may be a tendency to over-generalize.
  * **Band 8:** Sufficiently addresses all parts; well-developed response with relevant, extended, and supported ideas.
  * **Band 9:** Fully addresses all parts; fully developed position with relevant, fully extended, and well-supported ideas.
* **Examiner Secret:** Never change your mind in the conclusion or introduce brand new arguments that were not developed in the body paragraphs. Consistency is paramount for TR.

## Criterion 2: Coherence and Cohesion (CC)

* **What Examiners Look For:**
  * Logical progression of ideas and paragraphing.
  * Clear central topic within each paragraph.
  * Appropriate and natural use of cohesive devices, referencing, and substitutions.
* **The Difference between Band 6, 7, and 8:**
  * **Band 6:** Uses cohesive devices effectively, but cohesion within and/or between sentences may be faulty or mechanical (e.g., overusing *Firstly, Secondly, In conclusion* at the start of every sentence); may not always use referencing clearly.
  * **Band 7:** Logically organizes information with clear progression; uses a range of cohesive devices appropriately, though there may be some under-/over-use; presents a clear central topic in each paragraph.
  * **Band 8:** Sequences information logically; manages all aspects of cohesion well; uses paragraphing sufficiently and appropriately.
* **Examiner Secret:** Top candidates achieve cohesion through pronoun referencing (*this phenomenon, these repercussions, such measures*) and lexical chains rather than relying solely on transition adverbs.

## Criterion 3: Lexical Resource (LR)

* **What Examiners Look For:**
  * Range and variety of vocabulary.
  * Accuracy in word choice, collocation, and word formation.
  * Sophistication: Use of natural, less common academic lexical items.
* **The Crucial Trap:**
  * Memorized idioms (e.g., *every coin has two sides*, *it's raining cats and dogs*) are penalized by examiners because they are colloquial and used unnaturally in academic writing.
  * Band 7+ demands accurate **collocations** (e.g., *mitigate the environmental impact*, *foster economic prosperity*, *exacerbate social inequality*).

## Criterion 4: Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA)

* **What Examiners Look For:**
  * Variety of sentence forms: simple, compound, complex, conditional, passive, and relative clauses.
  * Frequency of error-free sentences.
  * Punctuation control (commas, semi-colons, full stops, capitalization).
* **The Gold Standard for Band 7 vs Band 8:**
  * **Band 7:** Produces frequent error-free sentences; has good control of grammar and punctuation but may make a few non-systemic slips.
  * **Band 8:** The majority of sentences are completely error-free; occasional minor errors occur only as rare 'slips'.

## Penalty Rules & Word Count Policies

1. **Underlength Penalty:** Writing fewer than 150 words in Task 1 or fewer than 250 words in Task 2 directly reduces the Task Response score.
2. **Off-topic Penalty:** An essay that completely misinterprets the topic is capped at Band 4 or 5 for Task Response.
3. **Plagiarized / Memorized Answer Penalty:** If an examiner detects memorized passages from online templates, those sentences are disregarded from word count and penalized for lack of authenticity.
