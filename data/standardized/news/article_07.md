# IELTS Writing Task 1 Master Guide: All 6 Chart Types, Overview Strategies, and Band 9 Model Answers

IELTS Academic Writing Task 1 requires test-takers to write a factual report based on visual information. You have 20 minutes to describe, summarize, or explain information presented in graphs, charts, tables, diagrams, or maps, writing at least 150 words.

## 1. The Standard 4-Paragraph Structure for Task 1

Every high-scoring Task 1 report (Band 8.0 - 9.0) should follow a clean 4-paragraph layout:

* **Paragraph 1: Introduction (Paraphrase the Prompt)**
  * Rephrase the question using synonyms and varied sentence structure.
  * Formula: *The [type of visual] illustrates / compares / outlines / demonstrates information regarding [topic] in [location] over a period of [timeframe].*
* **Paragraph 2: The Overview (The Most Important Paragraph)**
  * Identify 2 to 3 main features, overall trends, highest/lowest points, or major differences.
  * **Golden Rule:** Do NOT include specific data figures, percentages, or dates in the overview. Numbers belong only in the body paragraphs.
  * Signal words: *Overall, it is readily apparent that...* or *Looking at the data from an overall perspective, it is clear that...*
* **Paragraph 3: Detail Paragraph 1 (First Group of Key Data)**
  * Group related categories together logically (e.g., categories with increasing trends).
  * State exact numbers, dates, units, and percentage points to support key comparisons.
* **Paragraph 4: Detail Paragraph 2 (Second Group of Key Data)**
  * Describe the remaining categories (e.g., categories with decreasing or fluctuating trends).
  * Highlight contrasts between the first and second groups.

## 2. The Six Visual Types in IELTS Task 1

### Type 1: Line Graphs (Changes over Time)
* **Focus:** Trends over a timeline (increases, decreases, fluctuations, plateaus).
* **Key Vocabulary:**
  * Upward: *rose significantly, increased steadily, witnessed a sharp surge, escalated.*
  * Downward: *plummeted, plummeted dramatically, saw a steady decline, dropped moderately.*
  * Stability: *leveled off, remained constant, plateaued at, hovered around.*
  * Peaks & Troughs: *reached an all-time peak of, hit a low point of.*

### Type 2: Bar Charts (Comparisons Across Categories)
* **Focus:** Comparing distinct groups, items, or countries at a single point in time or over years.
* **Key Structures:**
  * *Country A consumed nearly twice as much energy as Country B (X units compared to Y units).*
  * *While Category X constituted the highest proportion, Category Y accounted for the lowest share.*

### Type 3: Pie Charts (Proportions of a Whole)
* **Focus:** Percentages, shares, and sectors totaling 100%.
* **Key Phrases:** *accounted for, constituted, made up, represented, comprised, took up the lion's share of.*

### Type 4: Tables (Complex Data Grids)
* **Focus:** Identifying high/low extremes and grouping rows/columns rather than describing every single cell.

### Type 5: Process Diagrams (How something is made or works)
* **Focus:** Step-by-step linear stages or circular cycles.
* **Grammar Rule:** Always use the **Present Simple Passive Voice** (e.g., *The raw material is extracted, then it is filtered and subsequently packaged*).
* **Sequencing Linkers:** *Initially, in the subsequent phase, following this, once this is completed, finally.*

### Type 6: Maps & Floorplans (Changes in a Location over Time)
* **Focus:** Urban development, additions, demolitions, expansions, and conversions.
* **Key Verbs:** *demolished, knocked down, replaced by, transformed into, constructed, extended, modernized.*
* **Compass Directions:** *to the north of, in the south-western quadrant, situated adjacent to.*

## 3. Band 9 Model Answer & Examiner Breakdown

**Prompt:** *The line graph below shows the percentage of households with internet access in three European countries from 2005 to 2025.*

**Model Answer (182 words):**
The line graph delineates the proportion of households possessing an internet connection across three European nations—namely the UK, France, and Germany—over a twenty-year timeframe spanning from 2005 to 2025.

Overall, it is readily apparent that all three countries experienced substantial growth in household internet connectivity over the period. Furthermore, while the UK consistently recorded the highest figures throughout the vast majority of the timeline, France exhibited the most rapid acceleration.

In 2005, the UK spearheaded internet accessibility with approximately 40% of households connected, followed by Germany at roughly 32% and France at a modest 22%. Over the succeeding decade, connectivity in the UK rose steadily to hit 70% in 2015, maintaining a comfortable lead over Germany, which climbed to 61%.

By contrast, France witnessed a dramatic surge post-2010. By 2020, French internet access had caught up with Germany at 82%, before ascending to finish at an impressive 94% in 2025, marginally surpassing the UK (92%) and Germany (88%).

**Examiner Notes:** Task Achievement: Clear overview highlighting general upward trend and nation ranking; Coherence: Logical chronological progression; Lexical Resource: Natural collocations (*spearheaded, modest, comfortable lead*); Grammar: Flawless variety of complex structures and compound sentences.
