# Need-based Financial Aid

FINANCIAL SUPPORT

VinUniversity is committed to making world-class education accessible to talented students regardless of their financial background. Our Financial Support Programs help students and families overcome financial barriers and continue their learning journey with confidence. Financial Support is need-based, different from scholarships (which are merit-based).

FOR NEW STUDENTS (Incoming Applicants)

Applicants who meet VinUniversity's admission requirements — including both Vietnamese and international candidates — but face financial challenges may apply for Financial Aid (FA) to help cover tuition fees and continue their studies at VinUni.

The level of Financial Aid is determined based on the applicant's verified family financial circumstances. All applications are carefully reviewed and approved by the Financial Aid Committee.

Financial Aid results are announced on the 15th of each month.

Note: To ensure timely processing, applicants must meet both of the following conditions:

Have been admitted to VinUni and received their scholarship results.
Submit the Financial Aid application no later than 11:59 PM on the 15th of the previous month.

Applications submitted after this deadline will be reviewed in the next monthly evaluation cycle, and results will be released on the following 15th.
