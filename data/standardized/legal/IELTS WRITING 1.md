Sample Answers 
The line graph illustrates the number of 
participants taking part in five different activities 
at a social centre in Melbourne, Australia, over the 
period from 2000 to 2020. 
Overall, the film club consistently attracted the 
highest number of participants, while table tennis 
experienced the most significant growth. In 
contrast, amateur dramatics declined markedly . 
Musical performances saw a steady rise  from a 
low starting point, and martial arts figures 
remained largely unchanged. 
In 2000, the film club had around 65 members, far 
ahead of the other activities, followed by martial 
arts at roughly 35 and amateur dramatics at about 
25. Participation in table tennis was lower, at 15, 
and no one attended musical perfor mances. By 
2010, the film club had dipped slightly to 60, while 
martial arts fluctuated between 31 and 38. Table 
tennis remained stable at around 20 until this year, 
whereas musical performances began to grow, 
reaching approximately 12. Amateur dramatics, 
however, had dropped to 20. 
The following decade saw notable changes. Table 
tennis numbers rose sharply, overtaking martial 
arts in 2015 and reaching just over 50 by 2020. The 
film club regained popularity, ending at about 66
