# Master of Business Administration - Scholarships & Stipends

SCHOLARSHIPS & STIPENDS

Scholarships ranging from 50% to 100% for all outstanding candidates admitted to the program.

Note:

Scholarships cover tuition fees only and do not include expenses for study tours or additional coursework in Business or Management that may be required.
The scholarship policy applies to the 2024 academic year and is subject to change in subsequent intakes.
