# PhD in Computer Science - Scholarships & Stipends

SCHOLARSHIPS & STIPENDS

Full scholarships (100%) for all exceptional candidates admitted to the program, along with a stipend of 10,000 USD per year for those serving as Research Assistants or Teaching Assistants.

Note:

Full scholarships cover living expenses, health insurance, travel costs, and other allowances for all outstanding PhD candidates admitted to the program.
The scholarship policy applies to the 2025-2026 academic year and is subject to change in subsequent intakes.
